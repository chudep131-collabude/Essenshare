About This Data

Platform Principle #17: Impact statistics come only from confirmed completed transactions. A transaction is complete when the donor has confirmed the actual items and quantities handed over. Requested pickups that were never collected, cancelled transactions, or expired listings are excluded entirely. This makes the numbers conservative — and trustworthy.
