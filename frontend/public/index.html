How It Works

Three simple steps — no paperwork, no means testing, no barriers.

1

Donors list surplus

Individuals, restaurants, bakeries, and organisations post surplus food or useful products — free to collect, with full safety information.

2

Recipients find nearby

Anyone can browse donations near them. No account needed. Filter by category, distance, and dietary needs.

3

Pickup confirmed

A temporary single-use code confirms the handover. Inventory is updated instantly. Impact is recorded.

Built on dignity and trust
