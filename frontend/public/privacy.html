Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 — Written in plain language.

We collect the minimum information necessary to operate the platform. We never sell your data, and we never use it for advertising.

Who Operates This Platform

This platform is operated as a nonprofit. For data inquiries, contact us at: privacy@platform.org (placeholder — see your .env for the configured address).

What We Do Not Collect

  • Recipient identity (name, ID, passport)
  • Proof of poverty, income, or hardship
  • Recipient precise GPS coordinates (at rest)
  • Payment information (the platform is free)
  • Biometric data
  • Political or religious affiliation
  • Health or medical conditions
  • Race or ethnicity
  • National identification numbers

Location Privacy

When you search for nearby donations, your GPS coordinates are used to find listings and then discarded. They are not logged, not stored in the database, and not shown to donors. Donors never know where a recipient is located.

Donor pickup locations are public — this is necessary so recipients can find and collect donations.

Cookie Policy

We use functional cookies only. No advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies are set.

No cookie consent banner is required under GDPR guidelines for functional-only cookies. A simple informational notice is shown on first visit.
